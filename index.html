The story

Thirty years of code. Then AI bought me time.

I have built software for a living for thirty years. Systems, integrations, deadlines and late-night fixes. Good work, but it ate the evenings.

Then AI turned up as a colleague, and something unexpected happened: it gave me time back. The routine parts go faster, and the hours that used to vanish are mine again.

I have played games since the 80s, from cassette-loading home computers to today's phones. So I spend that time on the games I want to play myself: short, a little absurd, finished in a coffee break.

And there will be no ads in them. Not a banner, not a "watch a video to continue". Never.

Mosquito: Summer Bite

  • Android
  • Svenska · English · Suomi
  • Free, no ads
  • 3–5 min rounds

A hot summer day, a family garden party, and you: one small, hungry mosquito. Pick a victim, dive in with a Stuka howl, dodge hands, newspapers and dragonflies, then land and bite in three steps: inject, drill, pump. Fly home before the slap, and before the sun gets too high.

Want to train first? Sergeant Stingmire runs the Mosquito Academy. Earn your wings and you get the Academy Reflex, which saves your life once per day.
